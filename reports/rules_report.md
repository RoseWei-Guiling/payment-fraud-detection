# Rules champion

Generated by `scripts/build_rules.py`. Rules are in `rules/champion_rules.yaml`. Chosen on training months, judged on the validation month.

**How to read the numbers:** *decline rate* = share of all payments the rule blocks; *fraud caught* = share of fraud payments it blocks; *precision* = share of its blocks that are really fraud; *false-positive ratio* = genuine payments blocked per fraud caught.

## The rules in plain English

| rule                   | condition                                                                        | meaning                                                                      | why we tried it                                                                                                  |
|:-----------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| burst_24h              | `uid_n_24h >= 3`                                                                 | Customer made 3 or more payments in the previous 24 hours                    | Step 2 showed fraud rising from 1.85% (0 payments) to about 5% (2+). Draining and card testing look like bursts. |
| burst_1h               | `uid_n_1h >= 2`                                                                  | Customer made 2 or more payments in the previous hour                        | The same idea at a shorter, sharper window.                                                                      |
| unidentified_customer  | `uid_complete == 0 and TransactionAmt >= 100`                                    | No billing address or card start date, and the amount is 100 or more         | Payments with no customer ID are 11.2% fraud vs 2.5% (Step 3).                                                   |
| risky_email_domain     | `P_emaildomain_te >= 0.08`                                                       | Purchaser's email domain had 8% or more fraud in the training months         | Banks keep lists of risky domains; this builds the list from past fraud.                                         |
| risky_card_and_product | `card_type_and_product_te >= 0.10`                                               | This card network + debit/credit + product combination had 10% or more fraud | Some combinations (e.g. certain credit cards on product C) are far riskier.                                      |
| shared_device          | `uids_per_fingerprint >= 5`                                                      | Five or more different customers used this exact device setup                | One device behind many cards can mean a fraud ring.                                                              |
| emails_differ          | `email_P_equals_R == 0`                                                          | Purchaser and recipient email domains are both known but different           | Buying for someone else is a classic scam and resale pattern.                                                    |
| foreign_card_cents     | `amt_is_round == 0 and amt_cents not in [0.5, 0.95, 0.99] and has_identity == 1` | Odd pennies (e.g. 0.137) on a payment with device data                       | Odd pennies usually mean a currency conversion, i.e. a card from another country.                                |
| new_device             | `new_fingerprint_for_uid == 1`                                                   | A returning customer on a device setup they haven't used before              | The textbook account-takeover rule. Step 2 found the opposite pattern in this data.                              |
| big_for_customer       | `amt_to_uid_mean >= 5`                                                           | The amount is 5 times or more the customer's usual amount                    | The textbook "unusual spend" rule. Step 2 found almost no signal.                                                |
| night_time             | `hour <= 4 and TransactionAmt >= 200`                                            | Between midnight and 5am and 200 or more                                     | The workshop-game rule. Caveat: the dataset's clock and time zone are unknown.                                   |
